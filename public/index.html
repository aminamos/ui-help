State unemployment sites keep their own hours — Minnesota, for one, is only open Sunday–Friday, 6 a.m.–8 p.m. Central. UI Help takes your weekly answers 24/7, texts or emails you the exact filing for your approval, and queues it for the next window your state is open.

Pre-login build: everything works up to your state’s login page, in all 50 states plus DC, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. We never ask for your Social Security number or your state password on this site.

What we will never do here

Never type your Social Security number or state account password into UI Help’s signup, this chat, or any approval text. In this build, no filing is submitted to any state at all — approved work stops at the login page, on purpose.

UI Help is a private helper, not a state agency and not legal advice. Pick your state above for its official information and login links.
